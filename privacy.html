Privacy Policy

The Confidence File. Last updated 4 October 2026.

The short version. This is a private tool that uploads videos to one YouTube channel, its own. It has no users other than its operator, and it does not collect anything from anyone else.

Who this covers

This policy covers the software used to produce and publish The Confidence File, operated by Damilare Ayoola. The software runs on the operator's own server. It is not offered to the public, there are no accounts, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What it accesses

The software uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission. That permission is used for one thing: uploading finished videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.

It is authorised by the operator, for the operator's own Google account. It never asks any other person to sign in, and it has no way to reach another person's YouTube account or Google data.

What it stores

  • An OAuth token for the operator's own Google account, so videos can be uploaded without signing in each time. It is stored on the operator's server and is not shared with anyone.
  • The videos, scripts and research notes produced for the channel.

No personal data belonging to viewers or any other person is collected, stored or processed.

What it does not do

  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising or tracking, and no analytics on this site.
  • No cookies are set by these pages.
  • Nothing is read from any YouTube account other than the operator's own.

Services it relies on

Publishing a video involves Google's YouTube Data API. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

The operator can revoke the software's access at any time from Google account permissions. Doing so stops all uploads immediately and makes the stored token useless.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
