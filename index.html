Documentary Channel

The Confidence File

True stories about money, deception, and the people who believed.

The Confidence File is a documentary channel covering frauds, forgeries and impostors. Every story is drawn from court records and published reporting, and every subject is someone whose case is a matter of public record.

The channel uses AI-assisted narration. The research and the writing are checked against primary sources before anything is published.

About this site

This page exists for the software that publishes the channel. It is a private tool that uploads finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account. It is not a product, and there is nothing here to sign up for.

If you are looking for how it handles data, that is covered on the privacy policy page.
